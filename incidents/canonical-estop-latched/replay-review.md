## Known limitations

- The platform is **not** safety-certified. This replay review is engineering analysis material.
- Live bag replay requires Foxglove Studio + a recorded bag on a ROS 2 Jazzy host. CI runs do not exercise live replay.
- When the bag directory is missing the manifest reports `bag_status=missing_bag`; the review remains valid as a static incident report but the visual playback step cannot be performed.
- Markers without `sim_time_ns` are aligned to relative time only; the Foxglove playhead must be aligned manually using the surrounding context.
